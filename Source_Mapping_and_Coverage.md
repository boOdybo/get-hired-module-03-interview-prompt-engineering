## 2. Source wording preserved

### Source Prompt 1 — STAR answer
```text
Act as an interview coach. Generate a STAR answer for a fresh graduate in [field] answering: Tell me about a time you worked under pressure.
```

### Source Prompt 2 — Behavioural question bank
```text
What are the 10 most common behavioral interview questions for [sector]?
```

### Source activity — Prompt Duel
Pairs of participants write competing prompts for the same interview question. Both outputs are displayed side by side. The Guild votes on which prompt produced the better, more usable answer. The winning participant earns XP and explains their prompting strategy.

The slide deck retains the activity's instructional structure. Any added scoring rubric is explicitly labelled as a trainer-designed practice rubric, not an official XP scale.

## 4. Derived learning objectives

The source lists topics and activities rather than a complete measurable objective set. These objectives are therefore derived:

1. Identify the four parts of STAR in a behavioural answer.
2. Construct an interview-preparation prompt using Role, Context, Tone, Format, and Constraint.
3. Compare an AI-generated answer with an authentic personal answer and identify what AI structures well and what it cannot know.
4. Refine a prompt through at least one targeted follow-up instruction.
5. Save and organise at least five interview-preparation prompts.
6. Generate and organise a practice question bank for a chosen sector or entry-level role.
7. Explain why a prompt/output is more useful during Prompt Duel, using evidence rather than length or brand preference.

## 5. Additions and boundaries

The following are instructional enrichments, not source quotations:
- The fictional university sales-analysis example used to model STAR.
- The detailed five-part review criteria: Structure, Specificity, Authenticity, Evidence, Speakability.
- The editable Prompt Builder and five-entry Prompt Bank Builder.
- The sample follow-up prompts for clarifying a role, strengthening Action, checking authenticity, and making an answer speakable.
- The trainer-designed 10-point practice rubric for Prompt Duel.
- The take-home Interview Preparation Pack.

### Boundaries
- Do not invent a student's work history, achievements, tools, numbers, or results.
- Do not present AI-generated behavioural questions as a statistically verified ranking.
- Do not turn the session into a full interview-skills course; keep AI prompting and evaluation central.
- Do not attribute the English audio recording or vocabulary journal to the AI Tools session.
- Do not describe a fictional sample answer as a real ChatGPT output unless it was actually generated live and labelled with its context.
